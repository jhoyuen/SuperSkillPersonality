A little about the idea

This website was made to showcase a list of spells and weapons that are commonly available in role playing fantasy games. Here, you can choose a set of spells and weapons by dragging or tapping them into your loadout. The quick-select ribbon at the bottom keeps your choices close while you build a persona that reflects your play style.


How it works

STEP 1: QUICK SELECT - drag or tap a spell from the spell list or a weapon from the weapon list to quick select at the bottom.

STEP 2: MY SPELL LIST - move the spell icons in quick select into your spell slots.

STEP 3: MY WEAPON LIST - move weapon icons from quick select into your weapon slots.

STEP 4: MY PERSONA - choose "My persona" in the navigation to see your results.
